## Exploring Water Quality

Functions to summarise water quality data and create plots for further
analysis.

- [`summarise_exceedance()`](https://etc5523-2026.github.io/etc5523-assignment-4-abhignatippala/reference/summarise_exceedance.md)
  : Summarise how often water samples exceed a bacteria threshold after
  rain
- [`compare_sites()`](https://etc5523-2026.github.io/etc5523-assignment-4-abhignatippala/reference/compare_sites.md)
  : Compare swim sites by how much rain changes their water quality
- [`plot_exceedance()`](https://etc5523-2026.github.io/etc5523-assignment-4-abhignatippala/reference/plot_exceedance.md)
  : Plot the share of unsafe samples in each rainfall band
