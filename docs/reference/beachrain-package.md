# beachrain: Explore How Rain Affects Water Quality at Sydney Swim Sites

Provides Beachwatch NSW enterococci samples from 77 Sydney swim sites
(2015 to 2025) matched to rainfall in the previous 72 hours, functions
that summarise and plot how often samples exceed bacteria thresholds
after different amounts of rain, and a 'shiny' app that helps swimmers
judge how much rain changes the water at their beach.
